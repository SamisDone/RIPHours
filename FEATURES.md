# RIPHours: v1.2 Feature Expansion 🚀

RIPHours is a powerful, deeply integrated screentime blocker designed to help you regain control of your digital life. Version 1.2 introduces several key features to combat impulsive scrolling and foster deep work.

---

### 1. ⚡ Focus Surge (Pomodoro / Deep Work Block)

**What it is:** A 25-minute absolute block on all tracked sites, simulating a Pomodoro deep work session.
**How it works:**

- Activating "Focus Surge" from the popup hero section sets a timer.
- During this window, any attempt to visit a tracked site results in an **immediate redirect** to the block page, regardless of your remaining daily limit.
- A live countdown in the popup keeps you informed of your remaining focus time.

### 2. 🛡 Iron Will (Friction System)

**What it is:** A psychological barrier that prevents impulsive setting changes when your willpower is low.
**How it works:**

- If you attempt to **increase** a time limit or **disable** Hard Mode, the extension triggers the Iron Will modal.
- You must manually type the exact phrase: _"I am choosing to waste my limited time on earth doing nothing."_ before the changes are applied.
- This forced reflection helps break the cycle of "just 5 more minutes."

### 3. 🎯 Dynamic "Better Alternatives"

**What it is:** Replaces generic "Touch Grass" messages with your personal goals.
**How it works:**

- You can define a list of "Better Alternatives" in the Settings tab (e.g., "Read 10 pages", "Do 20 pushups").
- When a site is blocked, RIPHours randomly picks one of your goals and displays it as the call-to-action on the block screen.

### 4. ⚰ "Tombstone" Daily Recap Notifications

**What it is:** A nightly summary of your total time buried in tracked sites.
**How it works:**

- Every day at 9:00 PM, you'll receive a system notification summarizing your total "time sink" for the day.
- It serves as a gentle (or brutal) reminder of how much of your day was spent on these platforms.

### 5. 💾 CSV Data Export

**What it is:** Full control over your tracking history.
**How it works:**

- Export your 14-day history as a standard CSV file directly from the Settings tab.
- This allows you to perform your own analysis in Excel or Google Sheets.

### 6. ☁️ Multi-Device Cloud Sync Engine

**What it is:** Seamlessly synchronizes your settings and time tracking across devices.
**How it works:**

- **Instant Sync:** Settings, domains, and limits are synced across devices almost instantly.
- **Smart History Merge:** Uses a "Max Value" reconciliation algorithm to merge tracking time from different devices without hitting Chrome's strict storage sync quotas.
- Your limits are enforced globally across all your signed-in Chrome browsers.

---

### Additional Improvements

- **Precise Real-Time Enforcement:** We've bypassed Chrome's 1-minute alarm throttling with a high-frequency background check, ensuring blocks trigger the _exact second_ your limit is reached.
- **Improved UI/UX:** Formatted timers (HH:MM:SS), live active session labels, and a refined "Hero" section in the popup.
