Where do your hours go?

Pick the sites that eat your time. RIPHours counts what you actually spend on them and adds it up, in days of your life.

How it counts

  1. Only time you're there. A site counts while its tab is in front, the window has focus, and you've touched the page in the last minute or a video on it is playing with sound.
  2. Limits bite. Give a site a daily limit and, with hard block on, its tabs go to a block page until midnight. Loosening a limit means typing a sentence you won't enjoy.
  3. It stays here. Usage lives in this browser and is never sent anywhere. Only your sites, limits and settings sync, through your Chrome account.

Pin RIPHours from the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar. The badge shows today's total.
